🧬 Mii Mixer

Load two or more Wii Mii files (.mii, 74 bytes) and splice them into a new amalgamation.

1. Parents

Drop .mii files here or click to choose
Accurate render: off (you supply your own file; it never leaves your browser)

2. How to mix each part

Random: whole part from one random parent · Chaos: every setting from a random parent · Blend: sizes/positions averaged, styles picked randomly · or lock a part to one parent.

3. Result

Filename gets a new version number on every download. A fresh Mii ID is written. The .mii is the raw 74-byte Wii format; the .rsd is the same data plus the 2-byte checksum. Unknown bits and the creator name come from the first parent.
